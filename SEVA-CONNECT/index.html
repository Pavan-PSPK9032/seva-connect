Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start your journey.

Seva Connect is a volunteer management platform that connects volunteers with verified NGOs and events. It handles registration, attendance, hours, badges, certificates, leaderboards and impact tracking — all in one place. This is a demo project, so all data is stored locally in your browser.
Yes. Creating a profile, browsing NGOs and events, and registering are completely free. Our mission is to remove every barrier between intention and action.
Your Impact Score combines volunteer hours, events attended, NGOs joined and consistency streaks. You also earn XP that levels you up from New Volunteer 🌱 all the way to Community Leader 👑.
After logging attendance for an event, Seva Connect generates a verifiable Certificate of Appreciation with a unique Certificate ID. You can download it as a PDF and anyone can verify it on the Certificates page.
Seva AI analyses your stated skills, interests, availability and city, then cross-references them against open events and NGOs to produce a match percentage with clear reasons. Ask the 🤖 chat assistant anything!
Because this is a frontend-only demo, all personal data lives in your browser's LocalStorage — nothing is sent to a server. Never reuse a real password in demo applications.
